Robot controls

End-effector target

Pose of the fingertip point, midway between the finger tips, in the robot base frame. Roll, pitch and yaw turn about that point. Drag the gizmo at the gripper, jog an axis, or enter a target.

Target follows the current fingertip point.

Kinematics only; no collision checking. If a target cannot be solved, the arm stays at its last valid pose.

Saved poses & trajectories

Save the current arm, fingertip orientation and gripper opening. Moves start from the current pose and ease to a stop at each destination.

Saved in this browser.

Save a pose to plan a move.

Straight fingertip paths are checked before moving and may use a different arm configuration. Kinematics only; no collision checking.

Balance the ball

Track a target point or a drawn trajectory with the ball. The plate center stays fixed; only roll and pitch change, up to ±25°.

Click the plate or enter X/Y. Choose Center to return to the middle.
