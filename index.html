<publications_projects>

Publications & Projects

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) 2026

MonoFold: Monocular Sim-to-Real Manipulation of Deformable Sheets

Monocular perception-to-action pipeline (SAM2, Depth Anything V2, ResNet regressor) that compresses raw RGB into a compact 9-D corner-relative state, driving a mixture-of-experts RL policy trained in a multi-fidelity MuJoCo pipeline. 0.038 m real-world corner error vs. 0.031 m in simulation.

Sim-to-Real Deformable Objects Mixture of Experts MuJoCo
Co-authors: Raei, Zhao, De Momi, Ajoudani View Paper
AIM 2026

Sim-to-Real RL for Ball-Balancing Locomotion on Quadruped Robots

A single policy trained across 4,096 parallel Genesis environments, validated sim-to-sim in MuJoCo, then deployed on a real quadruped that walks while keeping a ball centred on its back using vision-based ball-state estimation.

Reinforcement Learning Sim-to-Real Quadruped Genesis MuJoCo
Co-first authors: Tian*, Raei* · Ajoudani, Trahanias View Paper
IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) 2025

RL Approach to Non-Prehensile Manipulation Through Sliding

Actor-critic DDPG framework for sliding manipulation with LSTM-based real-time friction estimation and domain randomization, achieving zero-shot sim-to-real transfer to a Franka Emika Panda.

DDPG LSTM Non-prehensile Zero-shot Transfer
Co-authors: Raei, De Momi, Ajoudani View Paper
RO-MAN 2026

Design and Evaluation of a Touchscreen-Based Teleoperation Interface for Robotic Manipulators

Touchscreen interface that maps continuous finger motion on a live camera view to manipulator motion for remote surface-contact tasks such as nuclear swab sampling. In a 20-participant study on a Franka Emika Panda, it cut median completion time by 53.5% vs. a joystick (2.50 vs. 5.38 min) and lowered NASA-TLX workload.

Teleoperation HRI Touchscreen Interface User Study
Co-first authors: García Cárdenas*, Kenan*, Raei* · Bremner, Giuliani, Ajoudani, Tapus View Paper
SSRR 2025

Low-Cost Rapid-Development Air-Ground Robotic Solution for Nuclear Power Plant Inspection

Aerial–ground robot team (3D-printed drone and Ackermann-steered ground vehicle with LiDAR, IMU and radiation sensors) built in three weeks with ROS2 and off-the-shelf parts for EnRicH 2025 at the Zwentendorf nuclear power plant. Field trials showed reliable SLAM without GNSS, mesh-network communication through heavy shielding, and real-time radiation monitoring.

Air-Ground Robots SLAM ROS2 Nuclear Inspection
Co-authors: Tian, Kuruppu Arachchige, Li, García Cárdenas, Raei, Dincer, Kenan et al. View Paper
BioRob 2024

Multipurpose Interface for Close/Far-Proximity MCR Control

Designed visuo-haptic interface with detachable mechanisms and visual-inertial odometry for mobile collaborative robot control, matching commercial MoCap performance at lower cost.

HRI Visual Odometry MCR ROS2
Co-authors: Raei, Gandarias, De Momi, Balatti, Ajoudani View Paper
ASCC 2022

Autonomous Landing on Moving Targets via Sensor Fusion

Developed 30Hz relative localization system using 1D LiDAR, camera, and IMU fusion. Implemented PID, fuzzy-PID, and LQR controllers for UAV trajectory tracking and landing.

LiDAR Camera IMU Kalman Filter PX4
Co-authors: Raei, Cho, Park View Paper
